## Information about Lille

**Travel.** Lille is very well connected by train. High-speed trains arrive at the two central stations, *Lille Europe* and *Lille Flandres*, which are a few minutes' walk from each other. Travel times are about 1 hour from Paris, 35 minutes from Brussels and 1h20 from London (Eurostar). If you are flying, there are several convenient options:
- **Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG):** direct TGV trains to Lille Europe in about 1 hour.
- **Brussels Airport (BRU):** take a train to Brussels-Midi, then a high-speed train to Lille (about 35 minutes).
- **Lille Airport (LIL):** a smaller airport with mostly domestic and some European flights, connected to the city centre by a shuttle bus.

**Getting around Lille.** The city centre is compact and easy to explore on foot. Nouveau Siècle is right in the centre, next to the *Rihour* metro station and a few minutes' walk from the Grand Place and both train stations. Lille also has an automated metro, trams and buses operated by [Ilévia](https://www.ilevia.fr/).
